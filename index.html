Thoughtful Care for Every Mind

From childhood developmental concerns to adult emotional well-being, Dr. Bhavani Kaushal provides compassionate, evidence-based psychological care tailored to individual needs.

Schizophrenia

Support for hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thinking, and emotional withdrawal.

Symptoms of Schizophrenia:
Schizophrenia is a chronic psychiatric disorder that affects thinking, perception, emotions and behavior. Common symptoms include hallucinations, delusions, disorganized speech or behavior, social withdrawal and reduced emotional expression.

Treatment Approach:
We provide comprehensive care including psychotherapy, evidence-based interventions and support for daily functioning.

Depression

Support for persistent sadness, low energy, hopelessness and loss of interest.

Symptoms of Depression:
Depression can affect mood, thoughts and daily functioning. Symptoms may include persistent sadness, fatigue, changes in sleep, changes in appetite and difficulty concentrating.

Treatment Approach:
Evidence-based approaches including CBT, DBT, ACT and other appropriate therapeutic interventions are used based on individual needs.

Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

Support for intrusive thoughts and repetitive behaviors that interfere with daily life.

Obsessive Compulsive Disorder:
OCD may involve persistent unwanted thoughts and repetitive behaviors performed to reduce distress.

Treatment Approach:
Psychological assessment and evidence-based therapeutic approaches are selected according to individual needs.

Anxiety Disorder

Support for excessive worry, panic attacks, fear and persistent anxiety.

Symptoms of Anxiety:
Anxiety disorders can involve excessive worry, restlessness, rapid heartbeat, sweating, difficulty concentrating and sleep disturbances.

Treatment Approach:
Therapy may include CBT, relaxation strategies and other evidence-based psychological interventions.

De-addiction

Support for substance and behavioral addictions to help build healthier habits.

De-addiction Support:
Support is provided through assessment, psychological intervention, coping strategies and individualized care.

Stress & Sleep Disorders

Managing chronic stress, burnout, insomnia and sleep-related concerns.

Stress and Sleep Support:
Therapy focuses on identifying emotional and behavioral factors that may affect stress levels and healthy sleep.

Bipolar Disorder

Support for mood changes ranging from emotional highs to depressive episodes.

Bipolar Disorder:
Support focuses on understanding mood patterns, improving emotional regulation and developing healthy coping strategies.

Child Psychiatry

Child Mental Health:
